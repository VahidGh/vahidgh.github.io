Biology & neuroscience

Mathematics

Physics

Robotics & control

Bio-inspired AI

MatNeuron

Quantum computing

My long-term vision: the next generation of scalable systems will join HPC and quantum computing, and open the way to quantum machine learning and new neuro-inspired AI. I study HPC as the first step.

Selected research work

  • OpenWorm Foundation · Senior Contributor (volunteer)

    Nov 2014 – Present

    I proposed and led ChannelWorm (2015–2018): I curated patch-clamp data from papers, fitted Hodgkin–Huxley models, estimated kinetics for channels with no data, and built the Python APIs and validation tests used across OpenWorm.

    ChannelWorm

  • wormsim2

    2025 – Present

    A real-time C. elegans simulator: a Hodgkin–Huxley neural model joined to a finite-element body. The C++ core has CUDA, OpenCL and OpenMP back-ends, runs about 40× faster than serial, and runs on CINECA's Galileo100 with SLURM.

    3D viewer Code

  • chModeler & Chopen

    2018 – Present

    A compact ion-channel model (about 5 parameters) that fits a curated set of about 270 channels in seconds, with an ML predictor of channel kinetics from the amino-acid sequence. A proof of concept, still in development.

    chModeler Chopen

  • SC-PINN

    2026 · Politecnico di Milano

    A physics-informed graph neural network in PyTorch, with ESM2 protein-language-model embeddings, that predicts how ion channels open and close.

  • Research Assistant · University of Tehran

    Mar – Sep 2014

    Lab of Systems Biology and Bioinformatics: I predicted ion-channel kinetics from protein sequence with ML and sequence alignment, and built a dataset of more than 200 channels from the literature.

  • Smart pacemakers & ECG

    2019 – 2020

    I set up and mentored a student team from different fields to study smarter pacemaker and ECG algorithms, using Python and PhysioNet data.
